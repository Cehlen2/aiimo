About us.

AIIMO (AI in Maths Oxford) was started in Michaelmas 2026 by two Year 3 maths students who are new to AI themselves. We wanted somewhere maths students could work out together what AI means for the subject.

↑ Join the mailing list (opens in a new tab) See the termcard

The reading group.

A small group that meets regularly to read about new work in AI and mathematics and discuss it. You don't need to be an expert. None of us are yet.

Beforehand

A short reading

Usually around 30 minutes. A paper, an article or a result that's in the news.

→
60–90 minutes

Discuss, research, debate

Pull the result apart, try things out, and take a position you have to defend.

→
Afterwards

A one-page write-up

A short record of what we concluded. They're collected on the Write-ups page.

Talks, open to everyone.

Speakers who use AI in their mathematical research explain what they do. Anyone can come, in any year, whether or not you're in the reading group. Speakers will be announced on the Termcard.

What we explore.

Four big questions, chosen by the people who signed up.

Proof

How AI proves and checks maths

Proofs, formal verification and Lean.

Research

AI as a research tool

Conjectures, examples, and how far to trust it.

Practice

How AI changes doing and learning maths

Creativity, careers and teaching.

Ethics

Who gets the credit?

Attribution, authorship, publishing and access.

Questions.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. We're maths students working it out as we go. If you can read a maths paper, or want to learn how, you'll be fine.

Which years can join?

All of them. Talks are open to everyone, and the reading group is open to any year.

How much reading is there?

Short by default, usually about 30 minutes before a session, with some weeks lighter or heavier.

What is a first session like?

Skim the reading beforehand if you can. If you can't, come anyway and listen in. We talk it through as a group, nobody is put on the spot, and you're welcome to bring a laptop to try things out.

Do I have to come every week?

No. Come when you can. Each session stands on its own.
