What we do↓
fig. 1 · who we are

Who we are.

AIIMO is a student-run group for Oxford maths students who want to understand how AI is changing mathematics. We run two things:

Reading group

We share a short reading before each session, about 30 minutes' worth. We then meet for 60–90 minutes to discuss it, test its claims and argue about what it means. Each session ends with a one-page write-up.

Talks

Speakers who use AI in their mathematical work explain what they do. Talks are open to everyone, in any year.

This term the committee is trying out the format in two test sessions. Regular sessions start in Hilary 2027.
